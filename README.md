🚀 Featured Projects

⛓️ DAGCHAIN

A DAG-based blockchain ecosystem focused on decentralized infrastructure and utility.

DAGCHAIN is the larger ecosystem I am building around decentralized technology, with multiple layers designed to work together rather than as isolated products.

Ecosystem:

DAGCHAIN — decentralized blockchain infrastructure

DAGARMY — community, education and ecosystem growth

DAGGPT — AI layer / AI utility within the ecosystem

🌐 https://dagchain.network

🤖 DAGGPT

An AI-focused layer within the DAGCHAIN ecosystem, exploring how intelligent applications and decentralized infrastructure can work together.

🌐 https://daggpt.network

🎓 DAGARMY

A community / education initiative built around making AI knowledge and career opportunities more accessible.

The long-term vision is to create a practical learning ecosystem rather than another collection of generic tutorials.

🌐 https://dagarmy.network

🔐 Vaultix

A crypto-wallet concept inspired by the usability of mainstream Web3 wallets, with a focus on giving users a practical interface for interacting with digital assets and decentralized applications.

💾 DeVision

A decentralized-storage concept exploring how unused local disk capacity can become part of a distributed storage network.

Core idea: turn otherwise idle storage into useful infrastructure.

🖥️ RAVYA

A persistent, Jarvis-style AI assistant concept designed to live alongside the user's normal desktop workflow rather than being restricted to a chat window.

🧠 TAM

An experiment around offline / locally running AI digital twins, with an emphasis on reducing dependency on paid hosted APIs.
